#Python program-Numerical operations
A Python-based calculator designed to facilitate various numerical analyses. The Advanced Number Analyzer Calculator is intended to assist students and professionals alike in exploring properties of integers.


The calculator offers several key functionalities:
 	-It determines whether a given number is prime or composite.
 	It identifies all prime numbers within a specified range.
 	It performs prime factorization of input numbers, expressing them as products of their prime factors.
 	It recognizes perfect squares and perfect cubes, aiding in the simplification of algebraic expressions and understanding of number patterns.
 	Additionally, the calculator maintains a history of calculations, allowing users to review and revisit previous analyses.
